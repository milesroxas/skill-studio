## How to use this file

- `[ ]` is open. `[x]` is fixed on `main`: add the commit after the line.
- An issue that turns out not to hold: tick it and add "not a bug" with the reason.
- A fix people will notice also needs a line in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).
- When every line is ticked, delete this file.

Severity: **high** gives wrong scores or loses data. **med** misleads or breaks a step. **low** is an edge case or friction.

### Checked, and sound

- The model is pinned to `jev-1.14.0`, as TypeSafe advises once thresholds rest on its answers. On 2026-11-15 it is the newest model, and `0.7.0` the newest SDK. The alias `jev-latest` moves with each release, and nothing here uses it.
- TypeSafe bills the state once a request, however many questions are asked of it, and bills no output tokens. So one request for an output's rules and checks is the cheapest way to ask them.
- A choice with a "none of these" option is what TypeSafe's docs now advise against, in favour of a yes/no beside the question. J2 found the same.
- The editor's rule judge sends the rule and two lines either side of it, never the file.
- `TypeSafe-Danger-Zone-Images: true` goes with a request that holds a picture and with no other. TypeSafe calls pictures and Box a preview whose format may change between releases, and the SDK is pinned to `0.7.0`.
- The output's picture is in the state, sent once for every question, and a check's own picture is in that check's question: where TypeSafe's docs put each.
- A picture is drawn at 1568 px a side at most, the size TypeSafe says to send.
- A question Jev left unanswered throws. It is never read as a no.
- Two packs look at one picture of a page, and its hash is in what each was asked.
- Box is used nowhere. J19 is where it would be.
