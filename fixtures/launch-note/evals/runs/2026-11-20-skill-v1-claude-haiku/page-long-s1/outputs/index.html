Everything that changed in Ferry 4.0.

Ferry 4.0 is released on 1 September 2027. Below is every change in the release, in order, with the area each one touches.

Each change is on for everyone from the day Ferry 4.0 is released, with nothing to switch on. Read the full notes at https://ferry.example/4-0.
