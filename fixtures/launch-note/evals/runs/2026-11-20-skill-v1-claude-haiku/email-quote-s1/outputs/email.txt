Subject: Ferry 3.4: Reminders that people answer.

Reminders that people answer.

Ferry 3.4 is here. Two-way reminders let you reply to a reminder with yes, no or a new time, so the person who sent it knows where things stand without another message.

Quiet hours mean no reminder is sent between 21:00 and 08:00, so your evenings and early mornings stay yours.

Ferry 3.4 costs 12 USD a month.
