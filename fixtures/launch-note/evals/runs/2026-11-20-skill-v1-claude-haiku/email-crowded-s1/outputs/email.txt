Subject: Ferry 3.5: six ways to fill a quiet week

Six ways to fill a quiet week.

Ferry 3.5 gives you six ways to fill the open slots in your calendar for 12 USD a month.

Gap finder: lists every open slot in the next 14 days.
Last-minute offers: 20 percent off an open slot in the 24 hours before it starts.
Group bookings: book up to 8 places at once.
Gift cards: sold for any amount from 10 USD to 500 USD.
Repeat bookings: repeat every week for up to 12 weeks.
Busy-time report: a chart of the 5 busiest hours of each week.

"Our Tuesdays are full for the first time." Ines Varga, Varga Pottery
