Subject: Ferry 3.3: reschedule in one tap

Reschedule in one tap.

Ferry 3.3 makes moving a booking a one-tap job for the person who booked it.

One-tap reschedule: the person who booked can move their own slot from the confirmation.

Reschedule limits: you set how late a booking can still be moved.

The update is free for everyone on a paid plan.

"Nobody phones us to move a slot any more." Tomas Bell, Bell & Daughter Cycles
