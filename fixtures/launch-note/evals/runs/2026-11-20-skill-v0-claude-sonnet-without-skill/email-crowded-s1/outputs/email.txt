Subject: Ferry 3.5: Six ways to fill a quiet week.

Hi,

Ferry 3.5 is out on 9 June 2027. It has six new ways to fill the gaps in your week.

"Our Tuesdays are full for the first time." — Ines Varga, Varga Pottery

What's new:

- Gap finder: Ferry lists every open slot in the next 14 days.
- Last-minute offers: offer an open slot at 20 percent off in the 24 hours before it starts.
- Group bookings: one person can book up to 8 places at once.
- Gift cards: sell a card for any amount from 10 USD to 500 USD.
- Repeat bookings: a booking can repeat every week for up to 12 weeks.
- Busy-time report: a chart of the 5 busiest hours of each week.

Ferry is 12 USD a month.
