Subject: Ferry 3.3: Reschedule in one tap.

Ferry 3.3 is out on 18 May 2027, and it makes moving a booking a one-tap job for the person who booked it.

What's new

- One-tap reschedule: the person who booked can move their own slot straight from the confirmation.
- Reschedule limits: you set how late a booking can still be moved.
