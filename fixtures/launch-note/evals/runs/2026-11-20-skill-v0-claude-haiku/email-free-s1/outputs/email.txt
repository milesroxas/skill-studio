Ferry 3.3: Reschedule in one tap.

Ferry 3.3 makes moving a booking a one-tap job for the person who booked it. They can move their own slot straight from the confirmation.

Reschedule limits let you set how late a booking can still be moved.

The update is free for everyone on a paid plan.
