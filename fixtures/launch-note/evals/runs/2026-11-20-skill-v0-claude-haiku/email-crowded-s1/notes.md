# Notes: Ferry 3.5 launch email

Deliverable: outputs/email.txt (plain text, 114 words including the subject line, under the 120 cap).

## Choices made
- **Headline vs. product-first rule.** The skill asks for the headline to open the note and for the product name and version to come first. I satisfied both by making the first body line "Ferry 3.5: Six ways to fill a quiet week.", which starts with the name and version and carries the headline word for word.
- **Subject line.** "Ferry 3.5 is here" (18 characters), so it stays under 50 and does not repeat the headline.
- **Features.** Written as plain sentences, not list marks, since the email rules forbid list marks. All six feature names and figures come from the brief. The six features match the "Six ways" headline.
- **Price.** "12 USD a month", with the currency code as the rule requires.
- **Quote.** Used verbatim, with "Ines Varga, Varga Pottery" as given in the brief.
- **Closing block.** Release date, support address, signer, in that order, one per line.
- **Excluded.** The internal codename line is not used anywhere, and the words exciting, thrilled, delighted and proud are not used.
