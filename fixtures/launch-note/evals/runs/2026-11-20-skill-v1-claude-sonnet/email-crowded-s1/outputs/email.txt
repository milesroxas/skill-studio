Subject: Ferry 3.5: fill a quiet week

Six ways to fill a quiet week.

Ferry 3.5 is 12 USD a month.

Gap finder lists every open slot in the next 14 days.
Last-minute offers put an open slot at 20 percent off in the 24 hours before it starts.
Group bookings let one person book up to 8 places at once.
Gift cards can be sold for any amount from 10 USD to 500 USD.
Repeat bookings can repeat every week for up to 12 weeks.
Busy-time report charts the 5 busiest hours of each week.
